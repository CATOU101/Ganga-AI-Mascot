## 8. Source-Registry Inconsistencies

- `sources.md` provides thematic classifications but no stable `source_id` values; all inventory `source_id` values are therefore null.
- The registry names the eight missions and several supporting thematic groups, but does not enumerate every coded PDF filename. Those files are marked unresolved unless their title/filename provides a conservative thematic match.
- The repository uses `knowledge_base/raw_documents/GRBMP/`, while earlier processing specifications also describe `knowledge_base/raw/GRBMP/`. This inventory records the actual processed path.
- `46_57_Surface and GW Modelling of the GRB (2).pdf` is thematically registered as SELECT WITH FILTERING, but its full 99 pages require OCR/manual review.
