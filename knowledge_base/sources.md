# Knowledge Base Sources

This file will maintain the provenance and metadata of documents considered for the project knowledge base.

For each source, the project team should record its title, publisher or author, publication date, URL or identifier, access date, license or usage notes, and processing status where applicable.

This registry covers the Ganga Brain Knowledge Base only. Avatar and frontend materials are outside its scope.

## Classification

- **SELECT** — suitable for the Knowledge Base.
- **SELECT WITH FILTERING** — useful, but only selected sections or content should be ingested.
- **DEFER** — retain for later consideration; do not include in the initial Knowledge Base.
- **HISTORICAL** — useful context, but never present it as current information.
- **PROJECT REFERENCE** — useful for project understanding or design, not intended as user-facing Brain knowledge.
- **EXCLUDE** — do not include in the Ganga Knowledge Base.

## GRBMP Source Register

The GRBMP collection was published around 2015 and contains substantial data from approximately 2010–2014. Mission reports and supporting studies remain valuable, but dated measurements, counts, incidents, status figures and programme information must retain their source date.

- **GRBMP Main Plan and basin-wide framework** — **SELECT WITH FILTERING**. Keep goals, definitions, basin concepts, planning principles and cross-mission explanations. Filter dated status claims and programme progress.
- **Mission 1 — Aviral Dhara** — **SELECT WITH FILTERING**. Keep definitions, environmental-flow concepts and effects of flow alteration. Identify historical flow measurements as historical.
- **Mission 2 — Nirmal Dhara** — **SELECT WITH FILTERING**. Keep pollution-source classifications and explanatory material. Historical STP counts, treatment capacity and water-quality/status figures are not current.
- **Mission 3 — Ecological Restoration** — **SELECT WITH FILTERING**. Keep verified restoration, biodiversity, habitat and ecosystem material.
- **Mission 4 — Sustainable Agriculture** — **SELECT WITH FILTERING**. Keep agriculture–river relationships, sustainable practices and pollution/resource-management concepts. Filter highly specialized or historical statistics.
- **Mission 5 — Geological Safeguarding** — **SELECT WITH FILTERING**. Keep conceptual geological and geomorphological material. Filter highly technical material where appropriate.
- **Mission 6 — Basin Protection Against Disasters** — **SELECT WITH FILTERING**. Keep concepts concerning floods, landslides and basin disaster risks. Date historical statistics and incidents.
- **Mission 7 — River Hazards Management** — **SELECT WITH FILTERING**. Keep verified river dynamics, flood hazards, sediment dynamics and river-specific hazards.
- **Mission 8 — Environmental Knowledge-Building and Sensitization** — **SELECT WITH FILTERING**. Keep environmental-awareness, knowledge-building, sensitization and public-engagement concepts. Mark material that is primarily project or design reference.

### 13. Regional Studies

### 14. Historical / Status Reports

- **GRBMP status reports and historical assessments** — **HISTORICAL**; retain for context and retrieval, but label the period and never present status figures as current.
- **Historical flow, water-quality, sewage, infrastructure, forest-cover and programme-progress figures** — **HISTORICAL**; current answers require current authoritative sources.

## Dynamic / Current Information Policy

The Knowledge Base is primarily responsible for stable conceptual knowledge and clearly labelled historical knowledge. The GRBMP collection must not be used as the source for current STP status, current water quality, current river discharge or flow, current forest cover, current programme progress, current government schemes or policies, or current infrastructure status. When the project implements those integrations, current answers should be routed to authoritative current data sources or APIs, with their own provenance and access dates. Historical GRBMP figures may be retrieved only when the answer clearly identifies the historical period and source date.

## Source Selection Principles

- We are not bulk-ingesting every GRBMP PDF.
- Large size alone is not a reason to reject a document.
- A document can be retained while only selected sections are ingested.
- Historical information is retained when it provides useful context.
- Current-looking numbers from old documents must not be presented as current.
- Highly technical, duplicate or regionally repetitive material should generally be deferred unless retrieval testing shows it is useful.
- Source provenance must be preserved for every ingested piece of information, including title, author or publisher, publication date, identifier or URL, access date, license or usage notes, document section and historical/current classification.
